Four steps that escalate on their own, so you don't have to chase anyone. The sequence follows each member's oldest unpaid charge or installment, runs the steps in order with each one waiting its turn, sends each step once, and writes every step to the member's reminder history. Texts use your Twilio connection from Settings and the daily text limit. Muted members get no texts, but notices and officer review still apply.

If DuesDesk is closed at that time, the day's steps run the next time it opens.
