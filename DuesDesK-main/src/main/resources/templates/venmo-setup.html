Settings · Venmo email import

Set up Venmo email import

About 15 minutes, once. Venmo emails the chapter's Venmo account every time someone pays it. You'll forward only those "paid you" emails to a dedicated chapter Gmail, and DuesDesk reads that Gmail.

Shortcut: if you can change the email address on the chapter's Venmo account itself, set it to the new chapter Gmail from step 1. Venmo then emails that Gmail directly, so you can skip steps 4 and 5 (forwarding and the filter).
1

Create a dedicated chapter Gmail

  1. Go to accounts.google.com/signup and create an account just for dues, e.g. alphabeta.dues@gmail.com.
  2. Use a recovery phone and email the chapter controls, so the next treasurer can take it over.
  3. Don't use it for anything else. DuesDesk only needs to read Venmo's emails.
2

Turn on 2-Step Verification for the chapter Gmail

  1. Signed in as the chapter Gmail, open myaccount.google.com/security.
  2. Under How you sign in to Google, choose 2-Step Verification and follow the steps. Google requires it before you can create an app password.
3

Create an app password for DuesDesk

  1. Still signed in as the chapter Gmail, open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  2. Name it DuesDesk and click Create. Google shows a 16-character password once.
  3. Paste it into Settings → Venmo email import → App password (spaces are fine). This is not the normal Gmail password.
  4. If you later change the Gmail password, Google cancels app passwords. Create a new one and paste it in again.
4

Add the chapter Gmail as a forwarding address

Do this in the Gmail that receives the chapter Venmo account's emails (often the treasurer's Gmail).

  1. In Gmail, click the gear icon → See all settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP.
  2. Click Add a forwarding address, enter the chapter Gmail (), then Next → Proceed → OK.
  3. Google emails a confirmation to the chapter Gmail. Sign in to the chapter Gmail, open the email from Gmail Team ("Gmail Forwarding Confirmation") and click the confirmation link.
  4. Back on the Forwarding tab, leave "Disable forwarding" selected and save. Don't forward all mail; the filter in step 5 forwards only Venmo payments.
5

Create the filter that forwards only Venmo payments

  1. In the same Gmail as step 4, click the Show search options slider icon in the search bar.
  2. From: venmo@venmo.com
  3. Subject: paid you
  4. Click Create filter, tick Forward it to, choose the chapter Gmail, and click Create filter.

Filters only forward new emails. Payments from before today won't be forwarded, which is what you want: you've already recorded them.

6

Keep Venmo emails out of the chapter Gmail's spam

  1. Signed in as the chapter Gmail, create a filter with From: venmo@venmo.com.
  2. Tick Never send it to Spam and create the filter. DuesDesk doesn't read the spam folder.
7

Connect DuesDesk and test it

  1. In Settings → Venmo email import, enter the chapter Gmail address and app password, keep the host imap.gmail.com, turn the import on and save.
  2. Click Test connection. You should see "Signed in to … and opened [Gmail]/All Mail".
  3. Send the chapter a small Venmo payment from a member's account, wait a minute, and click Sync now. It should show up as applied or in Unmatched Payments.
8

Tell members how to pay

  • Each member has a dues code (e.g. DUES-JSMITH) on their profile. Ask members to put it in the Venmo note; DuesDesk then applies the payment to them automatically.
  • Or save a member's Venmo name on their profile exactly as Venmo shows it. When you assign an unmatched payment you can also tick "remember this Venmo name".
  • Anything that doesn't match exactly waits in Unmatched Payments. DuesDesk never guesses.
