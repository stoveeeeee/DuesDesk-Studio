Appearance

Choose how DuesDesk looks. System follows your device setting.

Desktop notifications

Alerts from your computer so nothing slips past: bills coming due, payment plan installments due today, and a missed daily backup. Each alert appears once, and the bell at the top of every page lists the ones still open.

Turn on the encrypted daily backup under Treasury policies for this alert to apply.

Text messaging (Twilio)

Connect your Twilio account to send live SMS directly from DuesDesk. Reminder templates and the dispatch schedule are on the Dues page; house announcements and the rent reminder template are on the Rent page.

Credentials are stored locally outside your exported save, with owner-only file permissions where supported. They are not encrypted; anyone with access to this computer account may be able to read them.

Venmo email import

Records Venmo payments automatically from "paid you" emails forwarded to a chapter mailbox. Checks on startup and every 10 minutes while DuesDesk is open. New here? Follow the setup walkthrough.

Payment links

Add the chapter's payment accounts. Reminder templates can then include a link that opens the app with the amount and a note already filled in, so members pay in two taps and the payment is easy to match. Use {{venmo_link}}, {{cashapp_link}} or {{paypal_link}} for one service, or {{pay_links}} for all of them. Zelle has no link, so it is listed by name.

Encrypted daily cloud-folder backup

Writes an AES-encrypted snapshot to a local iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, or Google Drive sync folder at 2:00 AM, and also when DuesDesk opens or closes if no snapshot was made in the last 20 hours (so a laptop that sleeps overnight still gets its daily backup). The generated key is protected by your system credential store. Record the recovery password separately so you can restore a synced snapshot on a replacement computer.

Automatic late fees

One non-compounding fee per itemized charge after its due date and grace period. Existing saves without due-dated charges are not assessed.

Social clearance

Who counts as cleared on the clearance list and meeting packet.

When dues-only is off, a member's house rent counts too: the resident linked to that member on the Rent page, or (for residents not linked yet) the one whose name matches. Active payment plans are considered in good standing only when every unpaid installment has a valid date and none is overdue.
